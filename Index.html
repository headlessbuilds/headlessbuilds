What this is

Every video starts with a problem on the desk

No intro music, no studio, no script. Just the work that was happening anyway, recorded as it went.

Some of it is physical — a bracket that ends a two-year annoyance, a printer that needs its bed relevelled again. Some of it is software — a utility that took an evening and saved a hundred more.

Nothing here is a tutorial. It's the build, the parts that failed, and the file at the end so you can skip straight to the version that worked.
